How MosquiScan Works

A simple workflow from image capture to physical output.

01
📱

Capture

Use a smartphone camera to capture an image of a possible mosquito breeding environment.

02
🧠

Analyze

The trained AI model analyzes the image and classifies it into two categories.

03
⚡

Indicate

The ESP32 receives the classification and activates the corresponding LED.

04
📍

Record

The result, image, location, reason, and date are stored in the inspection records.

ℹ️
Important:

MosquiScan identifies images that may represent potential mosquito breeding sites. The AI does not confirm the presence of mosquito eggs, larvae, or adult mosquitoes. Human verification is still required.
